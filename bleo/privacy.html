Bleo privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026 · applies to Bleo for Android, version 0.6.0 and later

In short: Bleo has no ads, no account, no analytics and no tracking. Your videos, settings and watch history stay on your phone. The only thing Bleo ever sends over the internet is a subtitle search, and only when you tap Find subtitles online.

Who makes Bleo

Bleo is made by Utkarsh Tripathi. Contact: capsuleutkarsh@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Bleo reads the list of videos on your phone through Android's media library to show them by folder. It keeps these in its own app storage, which no other app can read and which is removed when you uninstall Bleo:

  • where you stopped in each video (to resume and for Continue watching);
  • your settings, the folders you hid and the videos you put in the Private folder;
  • your Private folder PIN, stored only as a salted SHA-256 hash, never as the PIN itself;
  • thumbnails Bleo made, and subtitles you downloaded.

None of this is sent anywhere. Bleo has no servers.

The one thing that goes online: subtitle search

When you tap Find subtitles online in the player, Bleo sends a search to OpenSubtitles.com:

  • the film or series name, taken from the file name (you can edit it before searching);
  • the season and episode number, if the file name has them;
  • the language you chose;
  • the file's OpenSubtitles hash: a number computed from the file's size and its first and last 64 KB. The video itself is never uploaded.

When you pick a subtitle, Bleo downloads it from OpenSubtitles. OpenSubtitles receives your IP address, as with any internet request; its own privacy policy applies to that. Bleo sends nothing else, and nothing at all unless you search.

Permissions

PermissionWhy
Videos (media library)To find and play the videos on your phone and SD card.
InternetOnly for Find subtitles online.
Fingerprint / face (biometric)To open the Private folder, if you allow it. Android checks the fingerprint; Bleo only learns yes or no.
Keep awakeSo the screen doesn't turn off while a video plays.
Storage (Android 10 and older only)To rename, move or delete videos when you ask, and to save screenshots to your gallery.

Renaming, moving and deleting only happen when you choose them, and Android 11 and newer ask you to allow each change in their own dialog.

Children

Bleo collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If Bleo ever starts sending anything else, this page will say so before that version is released, with the date above changed.
