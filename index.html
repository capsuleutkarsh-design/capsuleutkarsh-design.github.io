About me

I'm a VFX artist based in Mumbai, working on compositing, roto and tracking.

Studios run on a lot of small, repetitive work - renaming plates, chasing files, asking who has the latest version. I build tools that take that work away, so artists can spend their time on shots.

Everything I make is free for studios to use.
