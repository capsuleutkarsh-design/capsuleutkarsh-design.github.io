Bleo (say BLAY-oh) is Old English blēo: colour, the look of things. It's the sister of Gleo, the music player.

Features

Everything a video player should do

Bleo runs on its own engine, built from mpv and FFmpeg with every decoder, so a file that plays anywhere plays here.

Playback

  • Every format: MKV, MP4, MOV, AVI, WebM, HEVC, AV1, VP9, ProRes, DNxHD, with hardware decoding.
  • HDR tone-mapped for normal screens, and every subtitle type (SRT, ASS, PGS, VobSub).
  • Gestures: brightness on the left, volume on the right (with boost to 200%), swipe to seek with a thumbnail preview, double-tap to skip, hold for 2×, pinch to fill.
  • Audio tracks, subtitle style and delay, speed 0.25–4×, A-B repeat, frame step, sleep timer, picture-in-picture and screenshots.

Library

  • Your videos by folder, like MX Player, with real thumbnails, even for ProRes.
  • Knows your series: S01E03, 1x03, "Episode 5" or "Part 2" play in order, and Next goes to the real next episode.
  • Select many to share, move, rename or delete together; sort by name, date, size or length.
  • Continue watching, search, grid or list, hidden folders.

Picture and sound

  • One-tap Enhance with a light sweep across the video.
  • Sound fixes: clear voice, noise reduction and even volume.
  • Analyze & fix sound listens to a few seconds and turns on only what the sound needs.
  • Find subtitles online from OpenSubtitles, matched to your exact file.

Privacy

  • No ads, no account, no tracking.
  • Nothing leaves your phone, except a subtitle search you start yourself (privacy policy).
  • A Private folder behind a PIN or your fingerprint, hidden from every other app.
  • Videos are only changed when you rename, move or delete them, and Android asks you first.

FAQ

Questions

Something else? Open an issue or write to capsuleutkarsh@gmail.com.

Which APK do I need?
  • arm64: almost every phone from the last several years. Start with this one.
  • armv7: older 32-bit phones. Use it if Android says the arm64 APK isn't compatible.

Bleo needs Android 8 or newer. Both are under All downloads, with SHA256SUMS.txt to check them.

Updating from 0.6.0 or older says "App not installed"

From 0.6.1, Bleo is signed with its own permanent key, and Android won't install it over the older test builds. Uninstall Bleo once, then install the new APK. This one time Bleo forgets its settings, resume points and Private folder list; no video is touched. Every later update installs straight over it.

Android or Play Protect warns me about the APK

Bleo isn't on the Play Store yet, so Android asks before installing it and Play Protect may offer to scan it. Allow installs from your browser or file manager, and if Play Protect stops you, open More details and choose to install anyway (the wording differs between phones).

How private is the Private folder?

Bleo moves private videos into a hidden folder, .bleo_Private, and renames them Movie.mp4.bleo. No gallery, Google Photos, other player or file manager shows them, and they open in Bleo only after your PIN or fingerprint. Moving is instant, even for huge files.

Uninstalling Bleo doesn't touch them: after reinstalling, open Private and tap Find hidden videos. Remove from Private puts each video back in its own folder. Android asks once for "All files access" so Bleo can move them.

It hides files; it doesn't encrypt them. And a video Google Photos already backed up stays in the cloud.

What does "Find subtitles online" send?

Only when you tap it: the film or series name, the episode number and a fingerprint of the file (a hash of its first and last 64 KB, never the video) go to OpenSubtitles.com. The subtitle is saved inside Bleo and loads by itself next time.

Is it really free?

Yes. Bleo is free to use and share under the UT Community Licence 2.0. It is not for sale; if you change it, keep the credits and send your changes back. Its video engine is LGPL and built in public at bleo-video-engine.
